This deed records a £175 transaction on 11 March 2024 between Manorial Title
Register Limited and James P. Howard, II. The property described in it is a
“Possessory Title of the Lord of the Manor of The Dungeon.” The deed invokes
the Law of Property Act 1925, Halsbury's _Laws of England_, and the concepts
_jus in re propria_ and _jura in re aliena_ in support of that description.

## What It Establishes

The document is direct evidence that the named company executed the stated
assignment on the stated terms. It records the parties, date, consideration,
and the rights the seller purported to convey.

## What It Does Not Establish

No earlier conveyance to the seller is recited or attached. The deed therefore
does not supply a documentary chain from the Lee-Warner family—the last
plausible historical holders identified by this site's research—to Manorial
Title Register Limited. Its legal characterizations are the seller's
assertions, not independent adjudications or HM Land Registry findings.

That distinction is why the instrument is published in full. A document proves
that its maker made the recorded assertions; it does not necessarily prove
those assertions. See the [state of the claim]({{ '/state-of-the-claim' | relative_url }})
for the wider evidence assessment.
