Manorial Title Register Limited issued this certificate on 11 March 2024. It
records James P. Howard, II, under number 262120/96/108960 in the register
maintained by that company.

Despite the document's formal presentation and the word “verification,” it is
not issued by the Crown, a court, HM Land Registry, or another public body. The
register named on the certificate is the issuer's private register. The
certificate therefore demonstrates what the company recorded after its own
sale; it does not independently establish the company's title or a continuous
succession from the historical lords.

This archive preserves it as part of the complete transactional record, not as
official recognition. For official context on manorial titles and registration,
see [HM Land Registry Practice Guide 22](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/manors/practice-guide-22-manors).
