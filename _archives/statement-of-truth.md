Stephen Brian Else, a director of Manorial Title Register Limited, signed this
declaration on 11 March 2024. It states the company's account of the manor's
history, asserts that the company owned the possessory title, and expresses the
signer's belief that there was no living heir to the historical manorial title.

## How to Read It

The document is evidence that a named individual made these assertions in a
signed statement. That is meaningful: it identifies who stands behind the
claims and the form in which they were made. But a statement of truth is not
independent verification of its contents. The document does not exhibit the
underlying genealogical search, probate records, conveyances, or other material
needed to test its conclusion about living heirs.

Current research has identified plausible Lee-Warner successors after the
nineteenth century and treats the disposition after Major Chandos Brydges
Lee-Warner's death in 1944 as an open question. The statement should therefore
be read alongside the [succession account]({{ '/lords-of-the-manor#the-open-succession' | relative_url }})
and the site's [evidence assessment]({{ '/state-of-the-claim' | relative_url }}).
