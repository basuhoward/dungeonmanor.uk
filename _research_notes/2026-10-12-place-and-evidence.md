This update adds to the history of the place and corrects the level of
certainty in two existing accounts. It does not announce a newly discovered
deed or a resolution of the later lordship.

## Additions to the History of the Place

The [gardens page]({{ '/gardens#restoration-2025-2026' | relative_url }})
now follows the 2025–2026 restoration through Canterbury City Council's
announcements and the contractor's account. It distinguishes the
[1 May 2026 reopening](https://news.canterbury.gov.uk/news/dane-john-gardens-reopens/)
from completion of the remaining works. No completion date for those
outstanding items is inferred from that announcement.

A new [surviving-estate page]({{ '/history/surviving-estate' | relative_url }})
uses [Historic England's entry for Dane John Works](https://historicengland.org.uk/listing/the-list/list-entry/1273536)
to connect the documentary history with a surviving building. The listing
is existing evidence newly incorporated here, not a new archaeological
discovery or a record of a site visit. Hasted's mansion-demolition account
and the later railway clearance are treated separately.

## Corrections to the Interpretation

The [ancient-mounds account]({{ '/history/celtic-origins' | relative_url }})
no longer states that the surviving mound indisputably predates Roman
Canterbury. It distinguishes the interpretations in the
[scheduled-monument record](https://historicengland.org.uk/listing/the-list/list-entry/1003780)
and the [Kent Historic Environment Record](https://heritage.kent.gov.uk/Monument/MKE5063),
including the latter's placement of the Bronze Age axe in companion mound B.

The [Lee-Warner narrative]({{ '/history/lee-warner-era' | relative_url }}),
[lords page]({{ '/lords-of-the-manor' | relative_url }}), and
[evidence assessment]({{ '/state-of-the-claim' | relative_url }})
now distinguish family succession, farm ownership, church patronage, and
the descent of the lordship itself. Catalogued leases remain leads pending
inspection. The later Tyberton family members remain candidates, not proved
holders; their proposed tenure is now expressly an open question in the
assessment. This is a correction to the inference drawn from existing
evidence, not the discovery of a contrary conveyance.

## What Remains to Be Done

The [research ledger]({{ '/research' | relative_url }}) keeps the later
descent open on both sides of 1944. Inspection of the leases, wills,
settlements, and property schedules is still needed. The modern transaction
file has not changed, and no missing link in its historical chain has been
supplied by this update. Earlier dated research notes remain available as
records of the project's previous assessments.
