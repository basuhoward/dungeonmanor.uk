---
title: The Lee-Warner succession extended to 1944
date: 2026-08-07
summary: The working succession was extended through the Tyberton branch, while the absence of a post-1944 conveyance was made explicit.
---

The published account formerly ended while the manor was still in Lee-Warner
hands in the early nineteenth century. Evidence assembled from Hasted, Ireland,
Bateman, census material, and archival catalogues now supports a longer working
reconstruction through the Reverend Daniel Henry Lee Warner, Robert Henry
Lee-Warner, and Major Chandos Brydges Lee-Warner.

## What changed

- The Tyberton Court branch was distinguished from the senior Walsingham Abbey line.
- Catalogued leases of Donjon Farm were identified among the Tyberton muniments.
- Robert Henry and Chandos were added as **presumptive**, not documented, holders.
- Chandos's death in 1944 was identified as the present end of the plausible historical chain.

## What did not change

No instrument has been found carrying the genuine lordship from the Lee-Warner
estate to Manorial Title Register Limited or any identified intermediate holder.
The longer reconstruction therefore sharpens the modern evidentiary problem; it
does not solve it.

## Consequence for the site

The succession now distinguishes direct evidence, supported inference, and the
open post-1944 question. Future evidence may alter any presumptive entry.
