The site's earlier pages were candid about uncertainty, but a visitor had to
assemble the complete assessment from the FAQ, archive, bibliography, and
succession narrative. The new [State of the Claim]({{ '/state-of-the-claim/' | relative_url }})
places the controlling propositions in one auditable sequence.

## The central distinction

There is strong evidence for a historical Manor of the Dungeon. There are also
genuine documents executed in 2024. The missing proposition is the bridge between
them: no public instrument presently connects the last plausible historical
holder to the issuer of the modern documents.

The existence of the manor and the existence of the modern transaction are
therefore recorded as established facts. Ownership of the historical lordship
through that transaction is recorded as unestablished.

## Revision policy

These findings are not frozen. A new deed, probate instrument, archival
discovery, or authoritative legal determination may change the assessment. When
that happens, the finding and the date of revision will be recorded rather than
silently replaced.
