The archive was expanded with public-domain books, articles, legal reports, and
maps central to the history of the Dungeon. These include the principal Hasted
accounts, Godfrey-Faussett's _Canterbury Till Domesday_, Plowden's report of
_Hales v. Petit_, Ireland's nineteenth-century continuation, and Bateman's
record of the Lee-Warner landowners.

## Why this matters

A source list is useful; access to the sources themselves is better. Housing the
texts and plans alongside the site's interpretation allows readers to test
quotations, examine context, and disagree intelligently.

## Remaining work

The printed record is only the beginning. Original deeds, probate material,
valuations, parish records, and family papers will be necessary to address the
largest gaps in the succession.
